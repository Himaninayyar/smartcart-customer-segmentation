How the segments are built

SmartCart doesn't guess segments from rules — it discovers them. 19 engineered features (income, spend, household, tenure, engagement) are standardized, compressed to 3 principal components, then clustered with K-Means (k=4, chosen via elbow/silhouette on the notebook side).

01 · Encode

Feature engineering

Age, tenure, total spend and total children are derived; education and living situation are bucketed and one-hot encoded.

02 · Compress

Standardize + PCA

19 features are scaled to unit variance, then projected onto the 3 principal components that capture most of the spread.

03 · Cluster

K-Means, k = 4

Every customer is assigned to the nearest of 4 centroids in PCA space — the same math the live predictor runs per scan.
